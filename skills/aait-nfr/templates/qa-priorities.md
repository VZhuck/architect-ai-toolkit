**Priority scale** (suggested defaults for downstream AI skills; always overridable by context):

- **P0 – Critical**: assume it applies to every system; failure here is unacceptable.
- **P1 – High**: applies to nearly all non-trivial systems; explicitly de-scope if not needed.
- **P2 – Medium**: matters for most production systems; evaluate per project.
- **P3 – Situational**: high value in specific domains, noise elsewhere.
