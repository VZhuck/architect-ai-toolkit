## Priority Taxonomy
- **Critical**: applies when directly impacts business continuity. This means the system must maintain these specific attributes or requirement to prevent severe financial, legal, or operational damage to the organization.
- **High**: applies when operations are severely disrupted, but a backup process or workaround exists. Increased operational costs or delayed revenue, but survivable in the short term.
- **Medium**: applies when day-to-day operations continue, but with slight inefficiencies. No direct revenue loss (no impact on org goals); minor internal resource cost to resolve.
- **Low**: applies when business runs as usual; the issue is cosmetic or convenience-based; no financial or legal risk.
