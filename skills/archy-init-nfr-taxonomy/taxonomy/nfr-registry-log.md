# NFR Registry Log

## NFR Trace Taxonomy

- **NFR ID** - autogenerated registry entry ID, `NFR-` followed by a zero-padded number (`NFR-001`). Once created it is never changed or reused, even when the entry is retyped or dropped. The ID never encodes a type.
- **NFR Type** - the section of the NFR document the requirement belongs to:
  - QAR - quality attribute requirement, categorized by `taxonomy_dir/quality-attributes.md`
  - BD - business drivers and goals, categorized by `taxonomy_dir/business-drivers.md`
  - ASM - assumption (an unverified statement, condition, or belief about technical, business, or environmental factors that is taken as true to move forward with designing an IT system)
  - CSTR - constraint (the permanent rules, boundaries, and limitations that restrict the design choices, technology stack, and implementation methods for a system)
  - TBD - type not yet decided; needs review
- **NFR Category** - the exact quality attribute or business driver name, verbatim from the taxonomy for the NFR Type
- **Verbatim evidence** - the exact sentence from the source, so a reviewer can check the reading against the original
- **Metrics** - the measurable target stated by the source (for example `p95 < 300 ms @ 500 TPS`). Empty when the source states none; never an invented value
- **Source** - where the requirement came from. A source file reference with the exact line (`file.md:L88`), `SAD.docx (SAD.docx.md:L42)` for converted Word files, or `notes.pdf p12 (notes.pdf.md:L310)` for PDF. `USER` when added during review, `SKILL` when proposed by the model
- **Interpretation** - reasoning for the NFR Type, NFR Category, Priority (when set) and Confidence
- **Confidence** - one combined score (0-100):
  - 95 - explicit statement, unambiguous category, measurable metric stated
  - 85 - explicit statement, unambiguous category; no metric needed (CSTR/ASM) or BD with money, date or market position
  - 70 - category inferred between candidates, or QAR without a metric
  - 50 - vague statement, nothing measurable
  - <40 - functional or outside the vocabulary (goes to `## Dropped`)
- **Priority** - from `taxonomy_dir/nfr-priorities.md`; set only when the source states a matching business impact, otherwise empty
- **Status** - tracks the record's state, to structure the conversation and enforce checks. Reserved statuses are:
  - Confirmed - confirmed by a human during review (highest confidence)
  - Auto - the skill reasoned with high confidence and all criteria are met (for example, the quality attribute is defined and measurable metrics are provided)
  - TBD - reviewed and agreed to keep in the journal, accepting that the record might not meet all formal requirements
  - TO REVIEW - the entry was identified but might not have all details
  - Drop - the entry was dropped; it lives only in `## Dropped`
- **Comments** - final comments the user asks to record, for example a summary of the user's reply during review

## Open Questions

Targets the sources never stated, values the sources contradict, and candidates that map to no catalog attribute. **These never enter the draft or the target document** — not as assumptions, not as TBD rows. They are resolved at a gate or they stay here. A proposed default fills a gap in a requirement a source states; it is never a new requirement. Status is `TO REVIEW` while open and `Closed` once answered, accepted, or its entry is dropped.
